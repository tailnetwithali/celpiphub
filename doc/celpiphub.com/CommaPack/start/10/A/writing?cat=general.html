CELPIP 10-12 Response Dear Professor Lee, I hope that you remember me from last year; I took your course in Applied Engineering, you assessed my project on Renewable Energy Systems and you sponsored my year in industry. Since graduating, things have gone well. I have recently applied for a job at a small company developing sustainable power supplies for remote communities. The interview went well and they seemed very interested in my final year project as it represents the most cutting-edge technology. However, they they expressed some concern about my ability to translate my academic knowledge into a workable, commercial product. They have therefore asked for a reference from you to support my application. So, I am writing to ask if you would be willing to write me a reference letter. If you are happy to do so, the letter would need to confirm that you felt that my project was technically viable and that I have the engineering skills to develop it into a workable product. It would also be helpful if you could describe my ability to work in a team and my ’soft-skills’, such as negotiating and communicating. Many thanks in advance, Jose Ford

CELPIP 7-8 Response Hi, So here are my reasons for keeping the swimming pool. First of all, I use this pool every day, along with a number of other residents. We also have parties here and swimming lesions for the young children in the building. I think that swimming is a better way to get exercise, as it is easier on your joints and bones because it is a low impact activity. CELPIP 10-12 Response Dear Apartment Manager, Thank you for your invitation to complete a survey regarding the issue of the swimming pool in the building. As a long-time resident of this building, I feel that the swimming pool is out dated and in much need of repair. In the time I have been a resident here, I have never used the pool facility and consider it to be unnecessary. It is my opinion that it would be far more beneficial to residents of the building if you were to consider installing a small gym instead of renovating the swimming pool. After speaking with a number of my neighbours, it seems that many would favour the gym instead of the swimming pool particularly due to the cold weather in Calgary often making the idea of swimming unappealing. I know that a number of residents would prefer to have gym here in the building so it would enable them to exercise at various times of the day and also cut down on the additional expense of an outside gym membership. I would like to thank you for your time and hope that you will consider my opinion when making your decision. Regards, Elizabeth
