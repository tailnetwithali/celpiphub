Read the following information.

You are a college student. Your school is offering $2,000 to one student: this money will be used to pay college fees. The school is inviting all students to explain why they should get this award.

Click here to view sample responses.

Write an email of about 150–200 words to the college. Your email must include the following points:

  • a description of what you are studying at college
  • why you are interested in your studies
  • a statement of how well you are doing at college
  • your future plans

I am a student of this college, John Doe and want to write this email to talk about the 2000$ budget from college. Actually I study electricity at college. I am the Bachelor degree student. I have started to study this major since 2023 and this is my last year. This major gives me informations about electricity powers and the safety of inside building. Because I am always the person lead other people and I have loved this major from my childhood, I want to continue it.

The reason I want studying is my interest and above all my favourite lesson type. Also when I was a child I am caring people’s a lot because many accident happened about electricity at houses becasue of lack of enough education about the field.

If I want to write about my scores and how well is my performance I am able to refer my grades in all of lessons especially the hardest ones. I haven’t got any scores less than 17 from 20 because when a person love something he should study and like to pursue because of everything.

At the end, I like to study the PHD to complete my course. And I am going to establish a workshop for poor people to talk about the dangerous problems of electricity if they don’t care a lot.

I hope you help me to get the 2000 dollars for my college payments.

Thank you very much because of your time.

Regards,

John Doe

Revised Celpip 7 - 8 response

Dear Professor Smith,

My name is John Doe, and I am writing this email about the recent award of $2000 that the school has offered to one of the student. Actually I want to give you a brief explanation of my course. I am studying business administration, a new course that has recently added to our college. I have learned many things, including business branches and trading course. Recently, I have taken a course on science and technology along with management to prepare for all necessary project types in this subject.

On the other hand, my effort show that I am very interested in this major due to some reasons. First, it is very trendy nowadays and has many clients around the world. Secondly, this is related to my background in science. So I’m very excited to learn and get instruction in this field. And about my performance, actually when I first entered to this college, I was not too hardworking, but currently I have strong skills in online applications and I think my grades can tell the story. So in the future, I would like to move into the digital marketing and have idea to create my own educational packages.

Thank you for your considerations, and I hope to achieve the 2000 dollars.

Sincerely yours,

John Doe

Revised Celpip 10 -12 response

Dear Professor Smith,

I hope this email finds you well. I am writing this email to express my interest in the $2,000 award recently announced by the school. I believe I would be a strong candidate based on my academic record and motivation. I am pursuing a Bachelor’s degree in Business Administration. This course has provided me with a great deal of business understanding, including marketing, management and finance. I have developed a strong passion for these subjects.

Moreover, I enjoy my studies for their strong real-world applications. The business world is evolving, and I am excited to contribute to its progress and growth. I believe my current education will equip me with the necessary talents to use in the dynamic environment.

In terms of my academic performance, I am proud to claim that I have excelled academically. I am recognised for my dedication and high grades, even in the most demanding tests. Looking ahead, I plan to pursue a successful career in marketing. I am particularly interested in digital marketing and I am confident with this success - indicating route, I am about to reach it as soon as possible.

Thank you for considering my request and I look forward to hearing from you soon. Sincerely,

John Doe

Revised Celpip 7 -8 response

To be honest, I would recommend changing the time of the evening classes from 3 hours to 1.5 in two days due to some reasons.

Firstly, with this change, we can spend more time with our families. For example I always arrive home in 7 p.m every Tuesdays and can’t have fun more with my little sister. And I am sure you have the same problem as me with this issue.

In the other hand, if you think deep, you can figure that we don’t have enough time to study for our next day’s exam and give less scores on the test. Therefore, we are making our parents disappointed and less happy about our future education in university. Since this term is the last one and we are going to entering the university. So it makes the matter more vital for us.For example last week I arrived late to house and only studied a little for the test. Consequently I didn’t achieve my expected score.

So, because of these logical reason, I believe changing the evening classes time is better for us. We will soon noticing the effects well.

Revised Celpip 10 -12 response

Personally, I would likewitch the current schedule from one 3-hour evening class per week to two 1.5 - hour classes in a week considering some strong reasons. Indeed, although our previously - planned schedule itself seems suitable as it takes one evening of our routine curriculum, dividing the class to two less times offers more benefits for the following reasons.

To begin, all students might agree that Tuesdays are especially difficult with the extra 3 hours. Nonetheless, if the school management shorten the duration to 1.5 hours a long with two sessions over two days, we are certainly able to save more time to concentrate on our next day’s both subjects and oral exams. Additionally, due to the evening time of our class implementation, we always stay outdoors late at night, aking our parents feel more anxious. Moreover, the decision of holding evening classes on two days possibly allocates more time to set aside with our beloved family. Imagine how emotionally effective it will be if we arrive at home soon!

Considering these reasons, I strongly intend not to preserve the current schedule and change it for the better as soon as possible.

Again this is my personal thought on the issue and I will support whatever decision is made by the authority.
