Practice Test A - Writing Task 1: Writing an Email

Time remaining: 27 minutes

Read the following information.

You ordered a pair of glasses online. However, the glasses that the company sent you are too big and they give you headaches.

Click here to view sample responses.

Write an email to the customer service department in about 150-200 words. Your email should do the following things:

  • Explain the problem with the order.
  • Describe how you feel about the company’s mistake.
  • Suggest how they can correct the situation.

CELPIP 4-5 Response Dear Customer Service,

I am writing this email because I have a problem with my new glasses. I bought them from your online shop last week.

The glasses are very big for my face. When I wear them, I get a bad headache. I feel very unhappy because I paid a lot of money and I cannot use them. I was waiting for them for a long time.

Please help me to fix this. Can I change them with a smaller size? If not, please give my money back. I hope to hear from you soon.

Regards,

CELPIP 7-8 Response Dear Customer Service Team,

I am writing to bring to your attention an issue with a recent order I placed on your website. I received my glasses yesterday, but unfortunately, they are not suitable for use. The primary problem is that the frames are much larger than described. Because they do not fit properly, they cause me severe headaches every time I try to wear them. I feel quite disappointed about this mistake, as I have always had positive experiences with your company in the past and I really needed these glasses for work. To resolve this situation, I would like to request an exchange for a smaller frame that fits correctly. If an exchange is not possible, I would appreciate a full refund. Please let me know the necessary steps to return the item.

Best regards,

CELPIP 10-12 Response Dear Customer Service Department,

I am writing to formally express my dissatisfaction regarding the prescription glasses I recently purchased from your online store. Upon receiving the product, I discovered that the frames are significantly oversized and do not align with the measurements indicated on your website. More importantly, the improper fit has resulted in persistent headaches, making it impossible for me to wear them. I find this error quite distressing, as it has not only caused physical discomfort but also delayed my ability to perform my daily tasks efficiently.

To rectify this situation, I suggest that you provide a replacement pair in a more appropriate size at your earliest convenience. Alternatively, if a suitable replacement is unavailable, I would like to request a complete refund of the purchase price, including shipping costs.

I look forward to your prompt response and a swift resolution to this matter.

CELPIP 4-5 Response I would prefer to start work at 8:00 a.m. and finish early on Fridays.

I think this new schedule is very good for everyone. First, I like to finish work early on Friday. If we finish at 12:00 p.m., I can have a long weekend with my family. We can go to the park or go shopping together.

Second, starting work at 8:00 a.m. is not a problem for me. I usually wake up early in the morning. It is only one hour earlier than before. I can finish my tasks faster when the office is quiet in the morning.

Also, many people travel on weekends. If we leave the office early on Friday, we can avoid heavy traffic. This makes me feel less stressed. I think most employees will be happy with this change. Thank you for asking for our opinion.

CELPIP 7-8 Response I would prefer to start work at 8:00 a.m. and finish early on Fridays.

In my opinion, the proposed change to the work schedule would be highly beneficial for the team. I personally prefer starting our shifts at 8:00 a.m. in order to leave the office by noon every Friday.

The primary reason for my choice is the improvement in work-life balance. Having a half-day on Friday allows employees to handle personal errands and spend more quality time with their families. This extra time makes the weekend feel much longer and more refreshing. Furthermore, starting work an hour earlier is a small adjustment that most people can manage easily. In fact, many people are more productive in the early morning hours when there are fewer distractions.

Lastly, finishing at 12:00 p.m. helps us avoid the peak rush hour traffic on Friday afternoons. This saves a lot of time and reduces the stress of commuting. Overall, I believe this new schedule will increase office morale and productivity.

CELPIP 10-12 Response I would prefer to start work at 8:00 a.m. and finish early on Fridays.

After careful consideration of both options, I am writing to express my strong support for the proposed transition to an 8:00 a.m. start time in exchange for finishing early on Fridays. I believe this adjustment would significantly enhance our overall organizational efficiency and employee well-being.

One of the most compelling advantages of this new schedule is the psychological benefit of an extended weekend. By concluding our professional obligations by noon on Friday, staff members are afforded a substantial block of time to recharge, which is essential for maintaining long-term productivity. Moreover, the shift to an earlier start time aligns with the natural peak of cognitive performance for many individuals. Utilizing that quiet hour between 8:00 and 9:00 a.m. could lead to more focused work and fewer interruptions.

Additionally, from a logistical perspective, an early Friday departure is an excellent strategy to bypass the inevitable weekend congestion. Reducing commute times not only contributes to environmental sustainability but also ensures that employees return to work on Monday feeling genuinely rejuvenated. For these reasons, I am confident that the benefits of the new schedule far outweigh any minor inconveniences of an earlier start.
