Practice Test B - Writing Task 1: Writing an Email

Time remaining: 27 minutes

Read the following information.

You work for a clothing company. Your company has a new style of coat.

Write an email to a magazine in about 150–200 words. Tell the magazine about the new style of coat. Your email should do the following things:

  • Provide information about your company.
  • Provide information about the new style of coat.
  • Suggest who might buy this coat and why.
  • Invite the staff at the magazine to write about the coat.

Dear head of Alp Magazine,

About the new design I can say it has a unique style with different colors and the quality of materials is wonderful. Furthermore it is make with many numbers in our other branches it is now popular among people. The special brand offers more comfortable coat for people because it is with cotton and provides a large space for all sizes.

According the statistics we want all groups of young and teens and old people to able to buy and wear it.Because it combines nice colors with better quality that everyone likes.

At the end, the company wants to invite you to the company for our introduction. We will be so happy to see you here and promise to provide the whole information about the produce line and the detaisl you ask.

Thank you for considerations and we wait to see you soon in our company.

Best Regards,

John Doe

Dear Alp Magazine,

I’m writing this email to introduce our new coat, an exotic style of raincoat. Our company, founded two decades ago, is well known for its new brand, especially coats and jackets. We are also known for our high quality and have begun a new design style in the industry.

Well, in our new style of coat, we have done perfectly to combine different colours for different preferences. To be honest, what makes it modern is the material we use, including silk and cotton together. This trend is a favourite of many people. Furthermore the coat is designed to be used in casual and formal ceremonies for both genders. It has a classic pattern and mostly young people can buy it. So we would like to see you in our company and the team is very eager to invite you for introduction day. We promise to provide any information you need about this brand.

We look forward to seeing you here with great respect.

Sincerely,

John Doe

Dear Alp Magazine,

I hope this email finds you well. I am writing to introduce our clothing company and share details about the latest addition to our collection, a modern coat designed to make a strong impact in the fashion world. We are a renowned clothing brand, known for our commitment to high qualities, style and innovation. We have gained adequate experience in meeting the needs of fashion-conscious individuals.

Let me currently introduce the coat in detail. Our professional design team has meticulously created a coat combining elegance and comfort. Made from the best materials, it is suitable for both formal and casual occasions.

We believe this trendy coat will be the target of many people, especially the youth and middle- aged groups. Its timeless design and attention to detail make it a must-have brand for those valuing both style and practicality.

At the end, we would be honoured to see your esteemed magazine in our company. Our team is happy to provide any information you need for an upcoming magazine feature.

Thank you for considering our request. I am looking forward to seeing you soon.

Kind regards,

John Doe

Revised Celpip 4 - 5 response

I would like to change the office from the paper-base condition to paperless because of some reasons. Actually I know that the whole company is affected by it but they can adjust to it after a while.

Firstly as you know company is spending so so much money for printers, but they cannot fixed because they are old and the fix process is impossible. So it is waste money and time as well. For example I remember last month we spent 100000 dollars useless. However this can be used for other need because we can already use the computer records save the document.

Secondly it is very harmful for the area and making the workplace so dirty and bad-look. Imagine everyday the workers and printing team are wasting many papers for printing. And at end they might like not them and put them away. So it is very dangerous and bad for the view of our place. Furthermore when we deleting the printers from our office it is consider a big change in internationally and we can be the first companies that did this change around the area or even the world.

So because of these reasons I believe it is great to have paperless company from much point.

Again this is my opinion and I will support the final decision.

Revised Celpip 7 - 8 response

Personally I think it is a better opinion to continue the office works without any printer anymore because of several reasons that I am going to explain in the following sentence.

At first, because management spends so much money for repairing the old versions of printer in office, it is recommended to forget about using them and replace them with the available technology of computer applications. For example last month the company spent around 10000 dollars for repair the chipsets of the printer but again it still didn’t work properly.

On the other hand, when we utilise papers in the workplace, we don’t know about the certain disadvantage we cause for the nature and our place. As you understand we always have many waste papers in the bins for nothing. However, we can reduce it and think more positive about the atmosphere and nature. Because the amount papers we use and throw away every week is not a small number, so we have to be careful.

Therefore because of these reasons I think it is more effective to have paperless office now. Again, this was my opinion and I am always with you to support hatever result is made in the company by the head manager.

Revised Celpip 10- 12 response

In my opinion, I prefer having a paper-free office with no printers due to numerous reasons. Honestly, although an office without a printer appears to be entirely unconventional, its benefits outweigh the negatives for several logical reasons.

To begin, this innovative theme is definitely against the rise in everyday garbage. Millions of tons of paper are wasted constantly with the aim of draft making and printing. Therefore, through this practical strategy, the staff are encouraged to utilise computer applications and no longer rely on printers. On the other hand, economically thinking, this method is able to save money budgets for the company. For instance, with no printers, there is no need to purchase cartridges, paper and other printer-related devices. Thus, after approximately a decade, the company will notice the financial benefits.

Considering the mentioned reasons, while the employees may initially find it cumbersome to get adjusted to a paperless office, in the long run they will certainly acknowledge the simplicity of the access to records on desktops rather than paper -based issues.

Again, this is my personal opinion and naturally I will support whatever decision is made.
