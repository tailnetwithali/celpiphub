MAN: Are we allowed to say anything? In a democratic society like Canada, freedom of speech and expression is a must. However, there has been a debate about whether we are allowed to say anything we want anytime.

Supporters of freedom of speech argue that an individual has the ultimate right to say anything he wants, while others believe there should be some restrictions. People from different walks of life, especially youngsters, have taken part in this debate.

Caroline Abigail, a senior credit manager at the Bank of Nova Scotia, quoted freedom of speech as one of the most fundamental rights guaranteed by the Canadian constitution.

She added that opinions can be held, expressed, and imparted as per one’s discretion. Though people should ensure that the reputation rights and freedoms of other persons and their private lives are not harmed.

Freedom of speech is in fact freedom from the absolute control of the government. That is people have the right to express themselves and have the government not punish them for it.

She emphasized the freedom of media and said that the press should be able to run without censorship or restraint by the government. In a similar tone, Nicholas Trammell, a hotelier from Winnipeg, categorically stated that people should be responsible for their words.

He believes that no one should be intimidated on account of his opinions, including his religious views, provided their manifestation does not disturb the public order established by law. He furthered that the free communication of ideas and opinions is one of the most precious rights of man.

Every citizen may accordingly speak, write, and print with freedom, but shall be responsible for such abuses of this freedom as shall be defined by law. Therefore, the state would have to set limits on this hypothetical freedom.

Nadia Khan, an economics graduate from the University of British Columbia, argued that Canada is a society where democracy is based profoundly on the rights of people to express how they feel.

She believes that it is a basic human right in all free societies and that it plays a vital role in the lives of people, especially how a country’s ministers take decisions for society.

In every country, she added that there is a different culture that shapes the society, And when people are insecure, they become emotional and will fight to protect their rights. Sometimes, such disagreement may lead to social unrest, which as a result can disrupt the economy.

Our society as such is where freedom of speech should only be allowed according to certain norms and values.
