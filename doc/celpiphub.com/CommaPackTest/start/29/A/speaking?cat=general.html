Yes for the people who aim to earn lots of money and get all the stuff that they desire, money could mean success. Besides, money does not usually fall into people’s laps easily. It has to be earned with lots of hard work and dedication. So, in that case it does make a person successful. For instance, Bill Gates, who is one of the richest people in the world, is considered to be quite successful and rightfully so. However, there are many other paths from where someone could earn money like inheritance or unfair practices and if a person has gained wealth from any of them, then he is not successful in my opinion. Moreover, there are a lot of people who are known and remembered for their work and not for the wealth they acquired. Mother Teresa is the biggest exemplification in this case. She is one of the most loved and revered people, even after her passing, because of all her good deeds. For them being good at their job is what makes them successful. Nevertheless, I feel a person is successful if he can accomplish all the goals that he set for himself, be it personal or professional. A person who is happy from within and living his best life is truly at the epitome of success.
