I strongly feel that physical education should be an essential component of the school curriculum. In today’s technology-driven world, where students either stick to their gadgets and have no time for physical activity, making PE or physical education a part of school time makes all the more sense. Physical education fosters the physical, moral, social, emotional, cultural and intellectual development of pupils. And this makes it even more essential as nowadays we see many pupils become overweight or obese because they get no time for any physical activity. The benefits of physical education are not limited to physical health only. It contributes greatly in stimulating mental, social, and emotional health as well. Physical education can prepare students with the skills they can use to make healthy choices throughout their lifetime. It cuts down the risk of addiction involving alcohol, tobacco, and other drugs. For all these reasons, I feel that physical education in school is a must.
