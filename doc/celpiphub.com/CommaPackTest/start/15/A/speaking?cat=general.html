In this day and age, when science and technology dominate the world, it wouldn’t be wrong to assume that scientists and engineers are more valued in society than musicians and painters. There is a growing need for capable workforce in fields of science and technology. This is the fundamental reason behind the increasing amount of people being attracted to these disciplines. With new developments taking place every day, escalating value is being attached to the work done by these professionals. It is often believed that their work is more productive because its primary aim is to make human life more comfortable.

The work of musicians and painters, on the other hand, appeals only a limited audience. There are only a few people who appreciate artistic and aesthetic value of things. For instance, one would find a limited number of people in an art exhibition as compared to the numbers in an auto expo. This tendency, however, is harmful for human beings. Caught in the bog mire of technological advancements, it is high time that human beings focus on the finer things in life. Due importance and appreciation should be given to the works of artists and painters as their works give an insight into the deeper meaning of life.
