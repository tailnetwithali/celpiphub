My mother constantly nagged me about every little thing, and to be honest, it was quite frustrating. However, now that I’ve grown up I realize and understand where she was coming from. The world out there is scary with people waiting to take advantage of the vulnerable. In my opinion, yes, the parents must supervise the teenagers, but it should not be extreme. Excess of anything is bad and in this case, this excess can push the child far away towards the activities that the parents want to prevent them from indulging in. Nevertheless, with the advent of social media, everyone is sharing every little detail of their lives on the internet. Given that it is a good way of connecting and communicating, it also gives the predators the requisite arsenal to prey on the immature children. Teenagers are at a stage in their lives where they are not children but also not matured adults. The decisions that they make can be rash and also be driven by peer pressure. I think that if the parents try to act as friends with their children and have a healthy and open line of communication, there would be no need for strict supervision as the children would feel more comfortable in sharing things.
