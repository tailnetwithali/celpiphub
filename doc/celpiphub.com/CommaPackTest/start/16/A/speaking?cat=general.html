I strongly feel that environmental studies should be an essential component of the curriculum in higher education. In this day and age, when man is confronted with a number of environmental issues such as global warming, pollution and climate change, it is imperative to educate the future citizens of the world about living responsibly and using resources judiciously. As children grow up, they tend to loose concern for the environment, focusing more on their careers and future job prospects. Therefore, it is important to make them aware of environmental concerns and what they can do to make a difference. With compulsory environmental studies education, a lot of changes can be initiated, both at the individual level and the community level. Students can start volunteering in projects and programs that are organized to control pollution, facilitate forestation and bring about positive environmental changes. Students can also help spread awareness to other members of the society by conducting workshops, performing plays and using the social media. A number of innovative ways can be employed to make people conscious of how their lifestyle affects the environment. Slowly but surely, this kind of education would help save the planet and ensure a better future for the generations to come.
