Voice5:

WOMAN: Thank you, mister Johnson, for speaking to us about professionalism in the workplace.

MAN: My pleasure. I’m so glad to be here.

WOMAN: So can you please tell our audience about the meaning of professionalism and how can people practice it?

MAN: Well, professionalism involves teamwork and collaboration. It serves as a vital component to define integrity and ethical standards of an organization.

WOMAN: Is this definition still relevant in today’s workplace environment?

MAN: You see, in today’s workplace, professionalism is perhaps over described and mostly misinterpreted, so it becomes difficult to explain it precisely. And as we are often asked to act professionally, we create our own definition of the expected perception. But in one of our studies, we found that utmost professionalism may lead to an increased number of unsatisfied employees, since they look for some kind of a leeway or look out for a less demanding work environment.

WOMAN: So is there a way out? I mean, how can we as employers deal with this?

MAN: Establishing a customary code of conduct plays a key role. It is an easy adaptation and would act as a guide to help employees become more professional. They can follow basic guidelines such as the dress code, office hours, timely submissions, basic do’s and don’ts. Now these are all self explanatory. One doesn’t think over it for too long. They can easily follow it and get to be called a professional employee.

WOMAN: So if an employee just adheres to a list of do’s and don’ts, will he or she be regarded as a professional?

MAN: Actually, no. To do one’s job well and to show up on time regularly doesn’t make you a professional. Instead, it goes beyond the individual’s sphere. It’s a collective endeavor. When made to pull off the common business objective, one’s progress contributes to everyone’s progress.

WOMAN: I think that way the entire organization would have a professional image. Are there any core elements of professionalism that our viewers need to be aware of?

MAN: Well, the right set of actions and presence comprise the core. Your personality and intellectual mind contribute equally. You should be reliable, competent, respectful, honest, and a believer of integrity. All these create a healthy yet competitive environment among the employees. It gives them a sense of responsibility besides taking off stress. This in turn leads to enhanced performance of both the individual and the group.

WOMAN: So much for professionalism.
